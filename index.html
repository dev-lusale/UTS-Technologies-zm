Map-Constrained Dead Reckoning (MCDR)

Problem addressed

Intelligent transport systems rely on satellite positioning. When the signal is blocked, jammed or distorted, vehicles disappear or jump on dispatch maps, leaving operators without a reliable location.

Solution

MCDR is an independent backup positioning layer. It estimates position from odometry and heading, and uses a particle filter constrained by known road geometry to stop the estimate drifting off the road.

Validation

Tested on a field dataset from a network RTK survey of Copperbelt University's Riverside Campus roads. Over roughly 850 m, MCDR's error was 2.55 m RMSE against 549.92 m for dead reckoning alone, and 88% of its estimates fell within the lane half-width.

Technology

  • Particle filter
  • Dead reckoning
  • Road geometry (LandXML)
  • Python / Jupyter
  • RTK GNSS survey data

Developed by Team KUDJOBA for the RUT International Project Competition (Russian University of Transport, 2026).

How we work with you

A clear, six-stage process so you always know where your project stands and what comes next.

Stage 01

Discovery & consultation

We start by listening: your goals, the people involved, your constraints and what success looks like for you.

You getA shared understanding of the problem and a clear brief.

Stage 02

Problem analysis

We look for the root cause rather than the symptom, studying existing processes, data and infrastructure before proposing anything.

You getA defined problem statement and the requirements a solution must meet.

Stage 03

Strategy & planning

We compare options, choose the approach that fits your budget and conditions, and agree scope, milestones and responsibilities.

You getA scoped plan with timelines and costs agreed up front.

Stage 04

Design & development

We design with your users and build in short iterations, sharing progress regularly so you can steer the work.

You getWorking increments you can review and test early.

Stage 05

Testing & delivery

We test against real data and real devices, fix what we find, then deploy and train your team to use what we have built.

Stage 06

Continuous improvement

After launch we monitor, support and improve the solution as your needs and your users' feedback evolve.

You getA solution that keeps delivering value over time.
