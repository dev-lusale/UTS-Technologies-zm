Privacy notice

This notice explains what information this website collects and how Kudjoba Solutions uses it.

Information you send us

When you submit the enquiry form we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, organisation, the service you are interested in, an optional budget range and your project description.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form service, which stores them on our behalf. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one setting in your browser, your light or dark theme choice, so the site remembers it on your next visit.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you sent us at any time by contacting us through the enquiry form.
